Decisions to make

1 · Where papers live
Recommend: arx-native (V2). Keep lavish for one-off reviews.
Papers are part of the ledger. Comments must survive sessions and sit next to hills.
2 · When a paper is written
Recommend: on promote and on hill close, the agent must write or update the hill's paper.
Rejections append to "What we tried". The project paper is written when you ask.
3 · One paper or many
Recommend: one living paper per hill, plus one project paper.
Alternative: one paper per champion (immutable, like a release note).
4 · Content rules
Recommend: the create-course rules in PROTOCOL.md: STE prose, poster per section, interactive charts, pop quizzes, decision-style tests, flip cards.
Plus ours: every number names slice and n, deltas are paired, negative results stay.
5 · Sharing
Open: loopback only, or a share step (static export with the thread)?
Client data rules apply. A paper is aggregate-only like rows, but examples need review.
6 · How the agent listens
Open: a background `arx paper poll` per session, or one poll across all papers?
Today: one paper per poll. A poll across papers is a small change.
